Habits in Saver track a recurring goal over a **streak period**, and keep a streak going as long as you meet the completion requirements you set.

## How a streak period works

- A streak period runs for a set number of days (for example, 15 days).
- You set how many total completions are required across the whole period (for example, 5 completions in 15 days).
- You can also require a minimum number of completions on each individual day within the period (for example, at least 3 that day).
- When you start a streak, you choose which day it begins on.

This lets you define anything from "every single day" habits to more flexible goals like "a few times a week, spread out."

## Using habits

- Log a completion from the app or the web app.
- Habits and their streak progress sync between every device once you're on a paid plan. See [Pricing](/pricing) for the free vs. synced limits.
- Without an account, habits stay local to that device and are unlimited.
