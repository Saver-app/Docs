---
title: Bookmarks
description: Save useful links in folders and keep them close to the work they belong to.
---

Bookmarks let you save links into folders inside a [space](/features/spaces), so they stay next to the todos and habits they're related to.

- Save a bookmark from the app, the web app, the [browser extension](/browser-extension) (without leaving the page you're viewing), or the Discord bot command `/create bookmark` or "Save to bookmark" message action.
- Organize bookmarks into folders per space.
- Bookmarks sync between every device and platform once you're on a paid plan. See [Pricing](/pricing) for the free vs. synced limits.
- Without an account, bookmarks stay local to that device and are unlimited.
