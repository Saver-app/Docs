---
title: Todos
description: Organized lists and tasks that stay close to your other content.
---

Todos in Saver are simple tasks organized into lists, kept inside a [space](/features/spaces) alongside your bookmarks and habits.

- Add a todo from the app, the web app, or a [Discord bot](/discord-bot) `/create todo` command.
- Todos sync between every device and platform once you're on a paid plan. See [Pricing](/pricing) for the free vs. synced limits.
- Without an account, todos stay local to that device and are unlimited.
