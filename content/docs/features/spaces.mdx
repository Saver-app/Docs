---
title: Spaces
description: Where your todos, bookmarks, and habits live, personal or shared.
---

A **space** is a container for todos, bookmarks, and habits. Every list, link, and habit belongs to a space.

- Spaces can be personal, just for you, or shared with collaborators.
- You can create multiple spaces to separate different areas of your life, e.g. "Personal" and "Work".
- Shared spaces have members who can all see and edit the content inside them.

## Sharing a space

Invite people to a space so you can manage shared todos, bookmarks, and habits together. Collaborators are counted as unique people across all of your spaces combined. See [Pricing](/pricing) for how many spaces and collaborators each plan includes.

## Offline spaces

You can create and use spaces locally on a device without an account, and that use is free and unlimited. An account and subscription are only required to sync spaces across devices or share them with collaborators.
