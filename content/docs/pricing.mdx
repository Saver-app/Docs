title: Pricing
description: Free and unlimited offline. Subscribe when you want to sync.
---

Saver is free to use locally, offline, on a single device, with no account required. Subscribe when you want your data synced across devices, spaces, and Discord.

## Plans

| Feature | Free | Plus | Pro |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Spaces | 2 | 6 | 20 |
| Synced todos | 75 | 750 | 3,000 |
| Synced bookmarks | 25 | 300 | 1,500 |
| Active habits | 3 | 15 | 50 |
| Collaborators | 2 | 5 | 10 |
| Custom Discord bot branding | No | No | Yes |

Collaborators are counted as unique people across all of your spaces combined, not per space.

<Callout title="Offline use isn't limited by plan">
  These limits apply to synced content. Content created locally on a device
  without an account is unlimited and always free, regardless of plan.
</Callout>

## Managing your subscription

- **iOS**: manage or cancel from your Apple ID subscriptions page.
- **Android**: manage or cancel from your Google Play subscriptions page.
- **Web**: manage your subscription from account settings in the [web app](https://app.saver-app.com).

For billing questions, refunds, or help choosing a plan, see [Support](/support).
