## Install Saver

Saver is available on:

- **iOS**: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6766563672)
- **Android**: Google Play
- **Web**: [app.saver-app.com](https://app.saver-app.com)
- **Chrome** and **Safari**: see [Browser Extension](/browser-extension)
- **Discord**: see [Discord Bot](/discord-bot)

## No account needed to start

You can open the mobile app and start adding todos, bookmarks, and habits right away. No account or subscription is required, and this local, offline use stays free and unlimited.

<Callout title="Local vs. synced">
  Content you create without an account stays on that device only. To use the
  same data across multiple devices, in the web app, or in Discord, you need
  an account and a subscription that covers sync. See [Pricing](/pricing).
</Callout>

## Create an account and sync

1. Open Saver on mobile or go to [app.saver-app.com](https://app.saver-app.com).
2. Sign up or log in.
3. Subscribe to a plan (Plus or Pro) if you want your data synced across devices, spaces, and the Discord bot.
4. Your todos, bookmarks, and habits now sync automatically between every place you use Saver.

## Spaces

Everything in Saver lives inside a **space**. A space can be personal or shared with collaborators. See [Spaces](/features/spaces) for details.
