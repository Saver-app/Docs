---
title: FAQ
description: Common questions about using Saver.
---

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to use Saver?">
    No. You can use the mobile app fully offline, on one device, without
    signing up. An account is only needed if you want to sync across devices,
    use the web app, use the browser extension, or connect Discord.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I go over my plan's synced limits?">
    You can keep using Saver, but new items past your plan's synced limit
    won't sync until you either free up space or upgrade to a plan with a
    higher limit. See [Pricing](/pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What platforms does Saver support?">
    iOS, Android, the web app at [app.saver-app.com](https://app.saver-app.com),
    Chrome and Safari browser extensions, a Discord bot, and the source-only
    [reMarkable mod](/remarkable). See [Getting Started](/getting-started).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share a space with other people?">
    Yes. Spaces can be shared with collaborators who can all see and edit the
    todos, bookmarks, and habits inside them. See [Spaces](/features/spaces).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do streaks work?">
    A habit's streak runs over a period you define, with a total number of
    completions required across the period and, optionally, a minimum per
    day. See [Habits & Streaks](/features/habits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    Manage or cancel from your Apple ID subscriptions page, your Google Play
    subscriptions page, or your account settings in the web app, depending on
    where you subscribed. See [Pricing](/pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I need help with something else.">
    Reach out through [Support](/support).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
