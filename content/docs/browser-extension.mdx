The Saver browser extension lets you save a bookmark or todo straight from whatever page you're on, without switching tabs.

- **Chrome**: available on the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/niippnpocnnlkdlmibpongjakhidfjhb?utm_source=item-share-cb).
- **Safari**: available on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6771103161) as a Safari extension.

## Logging in

Click the Saver icon in your browser toolbar and choose **Log in**. This opens the Saver web app to complete sign-in, then hands control back to the extension automatically. You don't need to copy any codes or tokens. Once logged in, the extension stays signed in using your browser's session until you log out.

## Saving from a page

With the extension installed and you're logged in:

1. Open the page you want to save.
2. Click the Saver icon (or use the extension's keyboard shortcut, if you've set one in your browser).
3. Choose a space and folder, then save it as a bookmark or todo.

You can also save a link without opening it: right-click the link and choose **Save link as bookmark**.

Anything you save this way syncs to your other devices if you're on a plan that includes sync. See [Pricing](/pricing).
