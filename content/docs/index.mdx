**Saver** is an app for keeping your todos, bookmarks, and habits in one place, on mobile, the web, in your browser, and in Discord.

It works fully offline and locally on your device for free. Subscribe when you want to sync the same data across devices, spaces, and platforms.

New here? Start with [Getting Started](/getting-started).
