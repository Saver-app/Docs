**Saver** is an app for keeping your todos, bookmarks, and habits in one place, on mobile, the web, in your browser, in Discord, and on reMarkable tablets.

It works fully offline and locally on your device for free. Create a free account when you want to sync the same data across devices and platforms, and upgrade when you need more room. See [Pricing](/pricing).

New here? Start with [Getting Started](/getting-started).
